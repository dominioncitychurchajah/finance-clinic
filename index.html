Questions people actually ask

Before you register.

These are the seven things people ask before they join a cohort. If yours is not here, message the number in the footer and a person will answer it.

Will I be asked to give money in the class?

No. There is no offering basket in a Finance Clinic session, and no lesson is locked behind a gift. Giving is taught as a subject, the same way debt and budgeting are taught as subjects. What you do with your own money afterwards is between you and God.

I do not attend this church. Can I come?

Yes. The teaching is rooted in scripture and you should know that before you walk in. You are not required to be a member of anything, and nobody will follow up about your church attendance.

Is 120 topics not too much? I work.

You are registering for six classes over ninety days, not for 120 topics. The full curriculum runs for years because it is a school, and schools take time. Most members decide about Stage 2 only after they have finished Stage 1 and seen something change.

I genuinely cannot pay anything.

Then you pay nothing, and you sit in the same seats, receive the same workbook and do the same assignments. Say so at registration in one sentence. No form, no interview, no committee.

What if I miss a class?

Every session is recorded and posted within 48 hours. The assignment still comes to you. Attendance is not the accountability engine here — the marked assignment is.

Do I need a smartphone?

No. Tick the printed workbook option at registration and your notes and assignments are handed to you on paper each month.

Can my children come?

Finance Clinic Juniors runs age-filtered material alongside the adult class. Children who learn to earn, save, give and invest before ten rarely have to unlearn anything later.
