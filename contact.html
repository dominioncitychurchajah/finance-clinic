Before you write

These four come up every time.

Do I have to pay anything to register?

No. Stage 1 has no fee. Registering reserves a seat and nothing else. There is no card form, no deposit and no invoice.

What happens after I send this?

Someone replies with the venue address, the date of your first class and the link if you are attending online. If you asked a question, they answer that too.

Will my details be shared with anyone?

No. Your details are used to send you class information and nothing else. We do not sell, share or publish them, and we do not add you to unrelated mailing lists.

Can I bring my spouse or a friend?

Yes, and it works better. Register separately so both of you get the notes and the assignments. Financial change lasts longer when the person you share money with is in the same room.
