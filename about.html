Why this exists

Two halves of the same answer, taught in two different buildings.

In one building people learn what scripture says about money — giving, honesty, contentment, provision. In another, people learn what a balance sheet is, how inflation works, and where to open an investment account. Most people only ever get access to one, and the half they receive cannot carry the weight of the whole problem.

Faith without arithmetic stays a wish. A believer who gives faithfully and has never written down what they owe is not more spiritual — they are undiagnosed. Arithmetic without conviction never survives a hard month. A perfect budget collapses the first time fear or shame gets a vote.

So we teach both, in the same room, on the same night, to the same people. Not as a compromise between them. As the actual answer.

Our culture

Three rules that hold the room together.

Everything else about how a Finance Clinic session runs comes out of these.

01

We do not compare. We compete with our yesterday.

Nobody is shamed for where they are starting from. The market trader clearing one debt and the executive restructuring a portfolio are doing the same work, and the room treats them the same way.

02

Testimony is curriculum.

Real stories from real members carry as much teaching weight as any lesson we prepare. What worked for the person sitting beside you is evidence, and evidence is what changes behaviour.

03

We finish what we start.

The assignment system is the accountability engine. Without it, a class is information. With it, it becomes transformation. Attendance is not what we measure. Finished work is.

Access

Keeping the door genuinely open.

A school about money that quietly excludes people without money is not worth running. These are the specific things we do about it.

Financial

  • Stage 1 is free to everyone, always
  • Stages 2 and 3 are progressively priced with payment plans
  • Every ten paying members sponsor one scholarship seat
  • Requesting a sponsored seat takes one sentence — no form, no interview, no committee

Physical and social

  • Classes held in accessible, central community venues
  • Virtual attendance available from the first cohort
  • Printed notes for members without smartphones
  • Finance Clinic Juniors runs alongside, so childcare is not a barrier

Cultural and language

  • Plain everyday language, no jargon without explanation
  • Stories and case studies drawn from Nigerian and African life
  • Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa delivery planned where a community is large enough
  • No assumed level of prior education

Where the money goes

A finance school should show its own books.

We teach members to know what they owe, what they earn and where it goes. It would be strange to ask that of a room and not do it ourselves.

Stage 2 and Stage 3 fees, together with sponsored-seat contributions, cover the cost of running Stage 1 for free. Members who go on pay for the members who are just arriving.
