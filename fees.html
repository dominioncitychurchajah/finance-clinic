Our promise

The No One Sits Alone Guarantee.

Complete your six assignments in ninety days. If nothing in your finances has moved — no savings started, no debt reduced, no plan written down — you get a private session with a facilitator, and then another, until something does.

You do not graduate alone, and you do not fail alone. That is the whole guarantee. There is no fine print because there is no fee to refund at Stage 1.

Questions about money

The awkward ones, answered.

I genuinely cannot pay anything.

Then you pay nothing, and you sit in the same seats, receive the same workbook and do the same assignments. Say so at registration in one sentence. No form, no interview, no committee.

Will I be asked to give money in the class?

No. There is no offering basket in a Finance Clinic session, and no lesson is locked behind a gift. Giving is taught as a subject, the same way debt and budgeting are taught as subjects. What you do with your own money afterwards is between you and God.

If Stage 1 is free, what is the catch?

There is none, and the maths is simple. Stage 2 and Stage 3 fees, plus the pay-it-forward seats, cover the cost of running Stage 1. Members who go on pay for the members who are just arriving.

Can I pay for someone else?

Yes. That is what a sponsored seat is. You pay for a seat, we assign it, and neither of you finds out who the other is.

What if I start Stage 2 and my situation changes?

Tell the facilitator. You move to a payment plan, or to a sponsored seat, and you keep your place. Nobody is removed from a cohort for falling behind on a fee.

Do I have to continue to Stage 2?

No. Many members finish Stage 1, put the plan to work for a year, and come back later. The door does not close.
