Questions about money

The ones people actually ask.

I genuinely cannot pay anything.

You pay nothing. You sit in the same seats, receive the same workbook, and do the same assignments. Say so at registration in one sentence. No form, no interview, no committee.

Will I be asked to give money in the class?

No. There is no offering basket in a Finance Clinic session. No lesson is locked behind a gift. Giving is taught as a subject, the same way debt and budgeting are taught as subjects. What you do with your own money afterwards is between you and God.

If Stage 1 is free, what is the catch?

There is none. The maths is simple. Stage 2 and Stage 3 fees, plus the pay-it-forward seats, cover the cost of running Stage 1. Members who continue pay for the members who are just arriving.

Can I pay for someone else?

Yes. That is what a sponsored seat is. You pay for a seat, we assign it, and neither of you finds out who the other is.

What if I start Stage 2 and my situation changes?

Tell the facilitator. You move to a payment plan, or to a sponsored seat, and you keep your place. Nobody is removed from a cohort for falling behind on a fee.

Do I have to continue to Stage 2?

No. Many members finish Stage 1, put the plan to work for a year, and come back later. The door does not close.
