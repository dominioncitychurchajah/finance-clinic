About

A clinic, because most people were never properly seen.

People are handed financial advice the way strangers hand out medicine — confidently, publicly, and without ever asking what is actually wrong. Finance Clinic exists because one sermon and a good intention cannot fix a room full of different problems.

Our vision

Finance Clinic · Lagos, Nigeria
Why this exists

The full picture, taught in one room.

In one place, people learn what scripture says about money. In another, people learn what a balance sheet is, how inflation works, and where to open an investment account. Most people only ever get access to one. And the half they receive cannot carry the weight of the whole problem.

Faith without math stays a wish. A believer who gives faithfully and has never written down what they owe is not more spiritual — they are unheard. Math without conviction never survives a hard month. A perfect budget collapses the first time fear or shame gets a vote.

So we teach both, in the same room, on the same night, to the same people. Not as a compromise. As the actual answer.

“Finance Clinic is not a seminar. It is a school. Every great school has a clear entrance, a clear curriculum, and a clear graduation — followed by a mandate to go and teach others.”

Our culture

Three rules that hold the room together.

Everything else about how a Finance Clinic session runs comes out of these.

01

We do not compare. We compete with our own yesterday.

Nobody is shamed for where they start. The market trader clearing one debt and the executive restructuring a portfolio are doing the same work, and the room treats them the same way.

02

Testimony is curriculum.

Real stories from real members carry as much weight as any lesson we prepare. What worked for the person sitting beside you is evidence. And evidence is what changes behaviour.

03

We finish what we start.

The assignment system keeps you on track. Without it, a class is information. With it, it becomes transformation. Attendance is not what we measure. Finished work is.

Access

Keeping the door genuinely open.

A school about money that quietly excludes people without money is not worth running. Here is what we do about it.

Financial

  • Stage 1 is free to everyone, always
  • Stages 2 and 3 are progressively priced with payment plans
  • Every ten paying members sponsor one seat
  • Requesting a sponsored seat takes one sentence — no form, no interview, no committee

Physical and social

  • Classes held in accessible, central venues
  • Virtual attendance available from the first cohort
  • Printed notes for members without smartphones
  • Finance Clinic Juniors runs alongside, so childcare is not a barrier

Cultural and language

  • Plain everyday language, no jargon without explanation
  • Stories and case studies drawn from Nigerian and African life
  • Yoruba, Igbo, and Hausa delivery planned where a community is large enough
  • No assumed level of prior education

Where the money goes

A finance school should show its own books.

We teach members to know what they owe, what they earn, and where it goes. It would be strange to ask that of a room and not do it ourselves.

Stage 2 and Stage 3 fees, together with sponsored-seat contributions, cover the cost of running Stage 1 for free. Members who continue pay for the members who are just arriving.

A full breakdown is published to members each quarter. Our full financial transparency report is being prepared and will be published here before the next cohort begins.
